About Dhanipurja Nepal

Connecting land buyers and sellers with total transparency and legal confidence.

Our Story

Dhanipurja Nepal was founded with a clear mission: to simplify real estate transactions across Nepal by providing verified, reliable, and hassle-free land purchasing options.

Focusing on key growth hubs like Danchhi, Bagdhara Chowk, Kageshwori Manohara, and the broader Kathmandu region, we bridge the gap between plot owners and potential buyers—ensuring every deal is built on trust and complete legal clarity.

Why Choose Us

Navigating land transactions in Nepal can be complex. Here is why our clients rely on Dhanipurja Nepal:

100% Direct Deals

We connect you directly to verified plot listings with no hidden costs or misleading information.

Prime Locations

Curated residential, commercial, and agricultural land plots with proper road access and solar exposure.

Expert Local Knowledge

In-depth expertise in local municipality regulations across Kageshwori Manohara and the Kathmandu valley.

Legal Verification

Your safety and peace of mind are our top priorities. Every plot listed on Dhanipurja Nepal undergoes strict verification checks prior to listing:

Lalpurja Clear

Verification of land ownership certificates (Lalpurja) to guarantee clean, unencumbered titles.

Map & Boundary Check

On-site measurement and survey map verification to ensure exact plot boundaries and road clearance.

Government Compliance

Assistance through official land revenue offices (Malpot) to ensure complete legal compliance.
